How SafeMeAI works

In plain words first, then the technology. SafeMeAI gives advice only and can be wrong. It is not a government or bank service.

SafeMeAI is a browser-based web platform with a public API. There is nothing to install: open safemeai.in on any phone or computer.

What happens when you check something

  1. You paste a message, link, UPI ID or number, or describe a call.
  2. Facts are gathered: does the website exist, how old is it, does it copy a bank's name, is it on a known-fraud list, is it listed by RBI or SEBI?
  3. Rules and AI work together: fixed rules catch known patterns. Google Gemini reads the message for tricks the rules miss.
  4. You get one clear answer in English or Hindi, with what to do next. Technical details are one tap away.

Why you can trust the answer (and when not to)

  • A website is called "verified" only after it is on our list of official sites and it really exists. A made-up name ending in .gov.in is flagged as fake.
  • Unknown never means safe. We say "not sure" and tell you what to check.
  • The AI can raise a warning but can never lower a proven danger.
  • Nothing here replaces your bank or the police. For any money matter, call your bank or 1930.
