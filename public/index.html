    PANIC CHECK

    Am I being scammed on this call?

    Answer these 6 quick questions. Tap Yes or No. The answer updates after every tap. Nothing you tap here is saved.

    1. Does the caller say they are police, CBI, customs, ED or a court?

    They may sit in a fake office with uniforms, flags or wireless sets behind them.

    2. Do they say a parcel, drugs or fake passports are linked to your Aadhaar or SIM?

    Often a FedEx or DHL courier story, or a money-laundering case in your name.

    3. Are they telling you not to hang up, or not to tell your family?

    They call it "digital surveillance" or "virtual custody".

    4. Are they asking you to send money to a "safe", "RBI" or "verification" account?

    They say your savings must be checked, or your accounts will be frozen.

    5. Are they asking you to share an OTP, Aadhaar, card or PIN details?

    No bank, police officer or government office ever needs your OTP or PIN.

    6. Are they asking you to install an app, or move to Skype / WhatsApp video?

    Remote-control apps let them see your screen and empty your account.

    Answer the questions

    Your result will show here

    Tap Yes or No for each question. If even one is Yes, do not continue the call.

    Warning level:
    This is a simple score from your answers, not a chance of being right.

    Remember these rules

    • No arrest over video call: Indian law has no arrest or questioning over Skype or WhatsApp.
    • No "RBI safe account": RBI and police never ask citizens to move money for "verification".
    • You can hang up: Nobody can punish you for ending an unverified call.

    WEBSITE CHECK

    Is this website real?

    Use this when you have just one website address (for example a bank link from an SMS). It shows the same plain answer as the main checker, plus optional technical details. Have a whole message, UPI ID or phone number? Use Check a message or link.

    Try an example:

    Website address

    How we check

    • Does the site really exist, and is it on an official government or bank address list?
    • Is the name a copy of a known bank or government site with small spelling changes?
    • Is it on public lists of known scam websites, or flagged by Google Web Risk?
    • Is it brand new, or does it hide where it really goes?

    Not being on a scam list does not prove a site is safe. When unsure, open your bank's official app instead of a link.
