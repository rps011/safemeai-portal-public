Privacy notice

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Last updated: 15 October 2026. SafeMeAI is a free prototype scam-advice service.

1. What we process

  • Text, links, UPI IDs or phone numbers you paste into the checkers, the AI advisor or the notice checker.
  • Files you upload to the notice checker (TXT, PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 5 MB).
  • Scam reports you submit (type of scam, the scammer's number, UPI ID or link, and an optional note).
  • Basic technical data your browser sends (IP address, browser type). The IP address is used for abuse limits and is hashed for reports.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, Aadhaar, OTP, PIN or passwords. Please never type them into SafeMeAI.

2. Where it goes

What you paste is sent to the SafeMeAI server in India (Google Cloud, region asia-south1). To produce an answer the server may check it against these services:

ServiceWhat is sent
Google Public DNSWebsite names found in your input
Cloudflare security DNSWebsite names found in your input
RDAP registriesWebsite names (to find how old a site is)
Google Web RiskLinks found in your input (when configured)
Google Vertex AI Gemini (asia-south1)The text you pasted or typed (when available)

The QR code on the Android page is generated by a third-party service (api.qrserver.com), which receives the download link. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Android app: the on-device checking claim applies to the Android app only. The website checkers send text to our server as described above.

3. Why (purpose)

Only to give you a scam-risk answer, to prevent abuse of the service, and to build anonymous community alerts from reports.

4. How long we keep it

  • Text you check is not stored. It is processed to produce your answer.
  • Community reports are kept only in anonymised form: the reported number, UPI ID or link, scam type and a hashed reporter identifier. We do not store your name or phone number.
  • Standard server logs (time, route, result category, no message text) may be kept for security and debugging.

5. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may ask for access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Because we do not collect your name or phone number, we may need the report details to find a record. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India after first using our grievance process.

6. Grievance officer

Email: support@safemeai.in

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under 18.

8. Changes

We will update this page and its date when practices change.
